## Research Notes & Next Steps

### How to Qualify Further
1. **Verify Sage 100 specifically:** Search LinkedIn for employees with "Sage 100" or "MAS 90/200" in their profiles at these companies
2. **Confirm retailer relationships:** Check each company's "Where to Buy" page for big-box retailer mentions
3. **Identify pain points:** Look for recent postings for EDI-related roles (hiring = growing pain)
4. **Contact strategy:** IT Manager or VP Operations are the right titles; alternatively, the CFO/Controller manages the EDI budget at this company size

### Warmest Leads (Prioritized for Outreach)
1. **NEXT Proteins** — Confirmed Sage 100 user, direct IT Manager contact known
2. **Rev-A-Shelf** — Active EDI Developer hiring = immediate pain, Home Depot supplier
3. **L.A.B. Golf** — Active EDI Specialist hiring = immediate need, fast-growing brand
4. **Doyle Shamrock Industries** — Sage 100 mentioned in job posting = confirmed platform
5. **SAV Eyewear** — Small team + confirmed Sage/EDI use = easy first conversation
6. **MRCOOL** — Director of Operations named in case study (Jarrod Greer) = warm contact available

### Companies to Disqualify
- Orderful case studies (Caraway, Darn Tough) use **Fulfil** or **Infor M3** ERP — not Sage 100
- Logicbroker case study (Grosfillex) uses **Infor M3** — not Sage 100
- Camco Manufacturing (1,000+ employees) — too large for Sage 100 (likely on a larger platform)
- Orgill, Inc. ($3.5B revenue, 1,000+ employees) — too large
